Backup & sync

Browser storage is per machine — your statuses and notes on Windows are not automatically on the Mac. Export here, then import on the other computer to move everything across.

The backup contains: job statuses & notes, company applied-flags & notes, manually added jobs, watched sites, your reference location, theme and active tab. Importing merges — imported values win on conflict.
