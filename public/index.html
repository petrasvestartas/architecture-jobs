Backup & sync

Your statuses, notes, manual jobs and watched sites are saved in this browser, on this device. The phone, the PC, the Mac — and the offline file copies — each have their own separate storage. To move everything to another device: Export here, then Import there.

The backup contains: job statuses & notes, company applied-flags & notes, manually added jobs, watched sites, your reference location, theme and active tab. Importing merges — imported values win on conflict.
